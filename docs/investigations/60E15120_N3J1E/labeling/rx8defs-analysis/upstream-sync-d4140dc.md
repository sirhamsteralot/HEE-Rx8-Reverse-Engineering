## 1. What moved

`RomRaider/rx8_defs.xml` (172 insertions / 208 deletions, 380 lines touched).
`RomRaider/logger_rx8_defs.xml`, `ECUFlash/60E0G500.xml`, `ECUFlash/RX8BASE.xml`
and everything else are blob-identical across the window. The other two
submodules did not move (MRP still `b243b35`, Rx8Man still `50f905b`).

Block-level parse of both revisions (15 `<rom>` blocks, all present on both
sides): the delta touches **no `storageaddress`** — verified by pairing every
removed `(name, storageaddress)` against the new revision: no table changed
address, and no same-name+address entry changed type/storagetype/size/category.

## 2. Collision audit (our defs vs the new pin)

def, reviewed copy, upstream snapshot, 9 per-ROM defs):

- **389 unique `(rom, storageaddress)` mappings to >1 table** →
  [`../defs-merge/axis_conflicts.json`](../defs-merge/axis_conflicts.json)

  `… [0x68724]`, injected literal-pool scalar entries sharing an address with

  non-dumped blocks). None was introduced by this pin move — the delta
  changed no storageaddress (§1), and the pin-tagged records are replaced

- **221 `upstream_pin_rename_alias` / orphaned-override records** →
  [`../defs-merge/inheritance_issues.json`](../defs-merge/inheritance_issues.json)
  (pin-tagged; the 311 legacy untagged records are untouched): our def files
  that keep the old name at an address upstream renamed in this window (195

  `per-rom/60E15120_N3J1E.xml` all keep local names by policy, see §3), plus
  the 26 `base_template_removed_at_pin` overrides from §1.
- **Removed-override coverage**: `0x0706be`/`0x0707e2` in the 60E0FC00 and
  60E0FB00 blocks are *not* covered by the regenerated per-ROM defs — and are
  not lookup descriptors in those ROMs' extraction either

  at those addresses), so no calibration coverage is lost; the removed
  upstream entries pointed at non-table bytes. The `G-ROM_FLEX`/`N3ZBEH`
  blocks have no dumped ROM. Recorded in the audit JSON
  (`removed_override_coverage`).
